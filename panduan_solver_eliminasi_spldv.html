Tips & Aturan Operasi Eliminasi

1. Samakan Koefisien

Untuk menghilangkan variabel, samakan angka koefisien variabel tersebut menggunakan perkalian (KPK).

Soal Langsung

Tentukan penyelesaian SPLDV berikut! Soal ①

①   x + y = 7

②   2x + y = 9

Pembahasan:
❶ Hilangkan variabel y
$$\begin{aligned} x + y &= 7 \\ 2x + y &= 9 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \\ \hline -x &= -2 \\ \mathbf{x} &\mathbf{= 2} \end{aligned}$$
❷ Hilangkan variabel x
$$\begin{aligned} \begin{array}{r@{}l|c|r@{}l} x + y &= 7 & \times 2 & 2x + 2y &= 14 \\ 2x + y &= 9 & \times 1 & 2x + y &= 9 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \end{array} \\ \hline \mathbf{y = 5} \end{aligned}$$
$$HP = \{(2, 5)\}$$
Tentukan penyelesaian SPLDV berikut! Soal ②

①   3x + 2y = 12

②   2x + 3y = 13

Pembahasan:
❶ Hilangkan variabel y
$$\begin{aligned} \begin{array}{r@{}l|c|r@{}l} 3x + 2y &= 12 & \times 3 & 9x + 6y &= 36 \\ 2x + 3y &= 13 & \times 2 & 4x + 6y &= 26 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \end{array} \\ \hline 5x = 10 \implies \mathbf{x = 2} \end{aligned}$$
❷ Hilangkan variabel x
$$\begin{aligned} \begin{array}{r@{}l|c|r@{}l} 3x + 2y &= 12 & \times 2 & 6x + 4y &= 24 \\ 2x + 3y &= 13 & \times 3 & 6x + 9y &= 39 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \end{array} \\ \hline -5y = -15 \implies \mathbf{y = 3} \end{aligned}$$
$$HP = \{(2, 3)\}$$

Soal Cerita

Kasus 1: Transaksi Barang

Ayu membeli 3 buku ($x$) & 2 pensil ($y$) = 16.500.
Budi membeli 2 buku ($x$) & 4 pensil ($y$) = 19.000.
Cari harga $1$ buku + $1$ pensil ($x + y$)?

Model SPLDV:

①   3x + 2y = 16.500

②   2x + 4y = 19.000

❶ Hilangkan y (Cari x / Harga Buku)
$$\begin{aligned} \begin{array}{r@{}l|c|r@{}l} 3x + 2y &= 16.500 & \times 2 & 6x + 4y &= 33.000 \\ 2x + 4y &= 19.000 & \times 1 & 2x + 4y &= 19.000 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \end{array} \\ \hline 4x = 14.000 \implies \mathbf{x = 3.500} \end{aligned}$$
❷ Hilangkan x (Cari y / Harga Pensil)
$$\begin{aligned} \begin{array}{r@{}l|c|r@{}l} 3x + 2y &= 16.500 & \times 2 & 6x + 4y &= 33.000 \\ 2x + 4y &= 19.000 & \times 3 & 6x + 12y &= 57.000 \quad \mathbf{(-)} \end{array} \\ \hline -8y = -24.000 \implies \mathbf{y = 3.000} \end{aligned}$$
$$x + y = 3.500 + 3.000 = \mathbf{6.500}$$

Latihan Mandiri

Tentukan penyelesaian SPLDV berikut:

①   x + y = 12

②   3x + y = 26

a. x = ... ?
b. y = ... ?
c. HP = {(..., ...)}
Kunci Jawaban:
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Hilangkan } y: \quad (3x + y) - (x + y) &= 26 - 12 \implies 2x = 14 \implies \mathbf{x = 7} \\ \text{Hilangkan } x: \quad (3x + 3y) - (3x + y) &= 36 - 26 \implies 2y = 10 \implies \mathbf{y = 5} \end{aligned}$$
$$HP = \{(7, 5)\}$$
